hackrf_sweep power is per bin, in dB relative to full scale (uncalibrated, like dBFS). Each line is the average of all sweeps since the previous one. There is no ADC level or clipping readout in scan mode: if strong signals look flat-topped, lower the gain. Mouse: wheel zooms, drag pans, click selects a signal.
